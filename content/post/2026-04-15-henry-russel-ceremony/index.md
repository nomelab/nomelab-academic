title: "Kevin Field Celebrates Henry Russel Award with the Michigan Community"

Prof. Kevin Field received his Henry Russel Award at a University of Michigan ceremony with Provost Laurie K. McCauley and President Domenico Grasso.

The occasion celebrated the research, teaching, and mentorship behind the recognition. Field thanked the students, staff, collaborators, mentors, and family members whose support has made that work possible. For NOME, the ceremony was also a chance to recognize the community that sustains our research and helps train future nuclear engineers.
