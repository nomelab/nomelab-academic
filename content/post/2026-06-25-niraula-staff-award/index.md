title: "Prashanta Niraula Recognized with NERS Staff Incentive Award"

Congratulations to MIBL accelerator scientist Prashanta Niraula on receiving a 2026 NERS Staff Incentive Award. The recognition celebrates his technical stewardship of the laboratory and support for students, researchers, and external users.

NERS highlighted Niraula’s work on ion beam preparation and cathode validation, including appreciation from graduate student Lingfeng Zhou. These contributions help researchers carry out reliable experiments and sustain a collaborative laboratory environment. We also congratulate fellow award recipients Shantae Neely and Abbey Sexton.
