title: "NOME and MIBL Launch Five Fusion Materials Projects"
---

NOME and the Michigan Ion Beam Laboratory are supporting five new fusion materials projects, with Prof. Kevin Field leading the University of Michigan contributions. Four projects are supported through ARPA-E’s CHADWICK program, and a fifth is part of the DOE Fusion Innovation Research Engine collaborative.

The collaborations address tungsten-based plasma-facing materials, vanadium alloys, layered materials for compact tokamak vessels, and advanced structural steels. MIBL will provide dual- and triple-beam irradiation experiments to investigate displacement damage alongside helium and hydrogen implantation. The work connects university, national laboratory, and industry partners working to improve materials for fusion energy.
