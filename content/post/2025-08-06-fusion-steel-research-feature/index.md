title: "Michigan Engineering Highlights NOME Research on Fusion Steels"
---

Michigan Engineering News has featured our collaborative research on steels for fusion energy systems. The work uses dual-ion irradiation to investigate materials under conditions relevant to the radiation challenges of future fusion reactors.

The feature highlights the contributions of Kelsy Green, Ying Yang, and their collaborators, together with the experimental capabilities that make these studies possible. Supported by the U.S. Department of Energy, this research helps connect alloy development with measurements of material performance under irradiation.
