title: "Kevin Field Receives Presidential Early Career Award"

Prof. Kevin Field has received a Presidential Early Career Award for Scientists and Engineers (PECASE) through the U.S. Department of Energy. The award recognizes early-career researchers for scientific achievement and leadership.

Field’s research combines irradiation experiments, advanced characterization, and accelerated analysis to understand materials performance in fission and fusion energy systems. The recognition also reflects the contributions of the students, staff, and collaborators who make this work possible. Congratulations to the entire team!
