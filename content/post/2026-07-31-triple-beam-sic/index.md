title: "MIBL Demonstrates High Temperature Triple Beam Irradiation of SiC"
---

The Michigan Ion Beam Laboratory has performed high-temperature triple-beam irradiations of chemical-vapor-deposited silicon carbide using silicon, helium, and hydrogen ions.

The experiments brought together Lingfeng Zhou, Zhexian Zhang, Hannah Hutton, and MIT collaborators including Sara Ferry and Keshav Vasudeva. Simultaneous delivery of the three beams enables experiments that combine displacement damage with helium and hydrogen implantation, supporting studies of materials in complex radiation environments.
