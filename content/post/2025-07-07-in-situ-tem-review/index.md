title: "New Review Examines In Situ TEM Ion Irradiation Facilities and Their Community"
---

A new collaborative review, “Transmission electron microscopy with in-situ ion irradiation: Facilities and community,” examines the capabilities and development of these specialized research facilities.

Prof. Kevin Field contributed to the review, led by Khalid Hattar and Jonathan Hinks. The article considers both the instruments and the international community that supports them. In situ TEM ion irradiation allows researchers to follow material changes during exposure, with applications extending from nuclear energy to semiconductors, quantum materials, and space technologies.
