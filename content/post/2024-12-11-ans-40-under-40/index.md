title: "Kevin Field Named to ANS Inaugural 40 Under 40 List"
---

Profs. Kevin Field and Aditi Verma have been named to the American Nuclear Society’s inaugural 40 Under 40 list. The recognition highlights contributions to nuclear technology, public engagement, and the development of future leaders.

Field was recognized for work on advanced nuclear materials, including accelerated irradiation experiments, materials aging, and collaborations that connect research with nuclear technology development. We congratulate both faculty members on this recognition of their contributions to the nuclear community.
