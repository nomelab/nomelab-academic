Kevin Field has been promoted to professor in the University of Michigan Department of Nuclear Engineering and Radiological Sciences. He joined NERS in 2019 and directs the Michigan Ion Beam Laboratory.

The promotion recognizes contributions to research, education, and service. Field’s team combines irradiation experiments and advanced characterization to understand how materials change in nuclear environments, supporting both fission and fusion energy research. We join NERS in congratulating Field and the other faculty members recognized in this year’s promotions.
