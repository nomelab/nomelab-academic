title: "UW–Madison Honors Kevin Field with Early Career Achievement Award"
---

Prof. Kevin Field has received a 2025 Early Career Achievement Award from the University of Wisconsin–Madison College of Engineering. The award recognizes alumni whose research, leadership, and educational contributions have made an impact early in their careers.

NERS highlighted Field’s work on radiation-tolerant materials, accident-tolerant fuel alloys, and methods that accelerate materials testing and characterization. The recognition also celebrates his mentorship and contributions to nuclear engineering education. Congratulations to Field on this honor from his doctoral alma mater.
