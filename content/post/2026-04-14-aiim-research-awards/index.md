Congratulations to Gabriella Bruno and Matthew Lynch on receiving Empowering Research with AI Awards from the AI Institutes at Michigan!

Matthew’s work uses lightweight, physics-based TEM simulations to generate synthetic training data for convolutional neural networks that detect irradiation defects. Gabriella’s research examines how human bias in labeled microscopy datasets can influence supervised machine-learning results.

The awards recognize complementary efforts to make AI-assisted characterization more useful and reliable for research on fission and fusion materials.
