title: "Kevin Field Receives the 2025 Henry Russel Award"
---

Prof. Kevin Field has received the University of Michigan’s 2025 Henry Russel Award, recognizing his contributions to research, teaching, and mentorship. He is the first faculty member in Nuclear Engineering and Radiological Sciences to receive the award.

The recognition reflects the work of the students, staff, and collaborators who contribute to NOME’s nuclear materials research and educational activities. We thank the NERS and Michigan Engineering communities for their support.
