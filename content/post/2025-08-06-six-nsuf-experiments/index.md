title: "MIBL to Support Six New NSUF Experiments"
---

The Michigan Ion Beam Laboratory will support six projects funded through the DOE Nuclear Science User Facilities Rapid Turnaround Experiment program. Together, the projects are expected to use more than 600 hours of accelerator time.

The studies address stainless-steel corrosion resistance, silicon-carbide grain boundaries, swelling in vanadium alloys, irradiation responses of stainless-steel grain boundaries and complex alloys, and silicon-carbide sensors for harsh environments. The awards bring university and industry researchers to MIBL to explore materials and technologies relevant to advanced nuclear systems.
